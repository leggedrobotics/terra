Controls & scene notes

Drag to orbit. Right-drag or two fingers to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. H returns home, T looks down, F follows the active machine, G toggles rich lighting (ambient occlusion and outlines) for slower graphics cards. P cycles the studio look, the plain paper style for figures and the stylized diorama; Capture PNG saves at twice the viewport resolution.

In manual play, arrows move and turn, Q/E turn the cabin, Space works, N waits, R resets. In replay, Space plays and arrows step.

Soil piles have slope-limited display shapes to prevent single-cell spikes. Their exact soil units remain in the cell inspector and recording; the slider changes only the display. Large rocks and containers mark static obstacle regions. The diorama's surroundings outside the fence, every flying clod, puff or dust cloud, and all arm and bucket motion between recorded states are illustration only. Click terrain or a prop to inspect its underlying cell.
