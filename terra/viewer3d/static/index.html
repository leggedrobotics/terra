Controls & scene notes

Drag to orbit. Right-drag or two fingers to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. H returns home, T looks down, F follows the active machine. P cycles studio, paper and diorama styles. G toggles rich lighting. Capture PNG saves the visible overlays.

Arrows or WASD move and turn; Q/E swing the cabin. Space digs or unloads, N waits, R resets. Undo restores the previous native state. In replay, Space plays and arrows step.

Green cells will be freshly excavated by Work at this heading. Amber cells are available after a cabin swing. Red cells are unavailable from this base in the current state; this does not prove they are permanently unreachable. Loaded machines must unload first. The purple outline marks the fixed precision band, including already dug edges.

Workspace and dumping layers are geometric previews. The native unload status includes the actual action checks. Soil mounds and arm motion illustrate discrete Terra actions; they are not physical trajectories or a bucket simulation.
